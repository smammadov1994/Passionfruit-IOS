How it works

When you go online, we pair you with one person who fits what you are both looking for: age range, distance and the preferences each of you set. The two of you meet on live video for 90 seconds. Afterwards each of you privately chooses whether to match. If both say yes, you can message each other and call again. If not, neither of you is told what the other chose. Quick intros work the same way in a shorter form: one person at a time on live video for 90 seconds, chosen by who each of you is looking for, and a match only if you both keep each other.

We cannot promise that a suitable person will be online when you are, or that you will get a match.
