House rules

Short dates, clear answers.

  • Four minutes a date

    Long enough to know, short enough to stay kind. Leaving early counts as a no from you.

  • Thirty-second turns

    The question card gives each of you the floor in turn, so nobody has to carry the conversation.

  • Sealed answers

    Yes or no, chosen privately. No answer in twenty seconds counts as a no.

  • A match opens the door

    Message each other, or video call again whenever you both like.

  • Adults only

    Passionfruit is for people aged 18 and over. You can report or block someone from inside any call.

  • Five dates a day, free

    Passionfruit Premium is $14.99 a month. It lifts the daily limit and adds dealbreaker filters and queue priority.
