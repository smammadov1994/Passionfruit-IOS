Questions

Is it private and safe?

Yes, and the app is built that way. Dates are live: we never record or store them. The app keeps date video out of screenshots and screen recordings, so they come out blank. Nudity is not allowed: during a date your own camera is checked on your phone, and the date ends at once for both of you if nudity is detected. Sexual or abusive language gets one warning, then the date ends. You can leave, report or block at any moment. The full rules are in the community rules and the privacy policy.

Why only New York City?

Live dating needs people online together. One dense city gives everyone a real chance of being paired the moment they go online.

When does it open?

No date is set yet. People on the list hear first, by email, borough by borough.

What do I need?

An iPhone. Passionfruit is an iOS app, and dates happen over video.

I live outside the five boroughs.

Not yet. The list checks your ZIP code and only accepts New York City addresses for now.

What do you do with my email and ZIP?

We use them to tell you when Passionfruit opens where you live. Nothing else.

Does it cost anything?

Joining the list is free, and so are five dates a day once Passionfruit opens. Passionfruit Premium is $14.99 a month: it lifts the daily limit and adds dealbreaker filters and queue priority.
