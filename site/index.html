Slide to meet someone
  1. Tonight

    Slide to meet someone.

    The home screen shows how many people you could meet are online right now. Slide, and Passionfruit pairs you with one of them. Keep scrolling, or drag the slider yourself.

  2. 1:30 on the clock

    Ninety seconds, face to face.

    You are on video straight away. A question card gives each of you a thirty-second turn, so nobody has to carry the conversation, and either of you can leave at any time.

  3. 0:00 on the clock

    Answer in private.

    Slide to say yes, or tap Not this time. You have twenty seconds, and the other person only finds out if you both said yes.

  4. Two yeses

    It’s mutual. Meet your dot.

    Every match makes a small character out of your two colours. It comes with you into the chat.

  5. In the chat

    It helps with what to say next.

    Your dot reads where the conversation stands and offers reply ideas, places you could go and the hours you are both usually free. You choose what to send.

House rules

Short dates, clear answers.

  • Ninety seconds a date

    Long enough to know, short enough to stay kind. Leaving early counts as a no from you.

  • Thirty-second turns

    The question card gives each of you the floor in turn, so nobody has to carry the conversation.

  • Sealed answers

    Yes or no, chosen privately. No answer in twenty seconds counts as a no.

  • A match opens the door

    Message each other, or video call again whenever you both like.

  • Adults only

    Passionfruit is for people aged 18 and over. You can report or block someone from inside any call.

  • Five dates a day, free

    Passionfruit Premium is $14.99 a month. It lifts the daily limit and adds dealbreaker filters and queue priority.

Questions

Before you join.

Why only New York City?
Live dating needs people online together. One dense city gives everyone a real chance of being paired the moment they go online.
When does it open?
Once 5,000 people are on the list. People on the list hear first, by email, borough by borough.
What do I need?
An iPhone. Passionfruit is an iOS app, and dates happen over video.
I live outside the five boroughs.
Not yet. The list checks your ZIP code and only accepts New York City addresses for now.
What do you do with my email and ZIP?
We use them to tell you when Passionfruit opens where you live. Nothing else.
Does it cost anything?
Joining the list is free and does not start a subscription. Five dates a day are free once Passionfruit opens. Premium is $14.99 a month, or $4.99 for founding members.
