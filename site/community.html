Community rules

In effect from 16 November 2026. These are the same rules shown in the app.

Adults only

Passionfruit is for people aged 18 and over. If someone seems younger, report them.

Video is checked automatically

During a date, a frame from your camera is checked for nudity every few seconds. If nudity is detected, the date ends at once for both of you.

Sexual language is not allowed

What you say is turned into text on your phone and checked. The first time you get a warning. The second time in the same date, the date ends.

Three strikes

Each violation is a strike. Three strikes pause your account while a person reviews it.

Leave, report or block anytime

You can leave a date at any moment, no explanation needed. Report and block are on every date and every match. Blocked people are never paired with you again.

Be kind

A no is a complete answer. No harassment, no pressure, no recording.

What happens when someone breaks the rules

  • There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour.
  • Every report is recorded and reviewed by a person. An account reported by several different people, or caught by our safety checks, is paused straight away until it has been reviewed.
  • Accounts can be suspended or removed. See the terms of use.

Tell us

The fastest way to report someone is the Report button in the app. You can also write to us.
