Support

How can we help?

We read every message and aim to answer within one working day. Reports about a person are looked at within 24 hours.

Write to us

support@passionfruitdating.com

Report a person

  1. During a date: tap Report on the date screen. You can leave the date at the same moment.
  2. After a date: tap Report on the screen that follows it.
  3. From a match: open the chat or the person's profile and choose Report, or Block to stop all contact.
  4. Outside the app: write to the address above with the person's first name and when the date happened.

Blocked people are never paired with you again. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number first.

Delete your account

In the app, open You, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your profile, photos, matches and messages. It cannot be undone.

Premium and billing

Premium is bought through Apple. To cancel or change it, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My account was paused

Three strikes pause an account until a person has reviewed it. If you think a strike was a mistake, write to us from the email on your Apple account and tell us what happened.

The waitlist

To leave the waitlist or change your ZIP code, write to us from the address you signed up with.
