Privacy policy

In effect from 25 November 2026.

The short version

  • We collect what is needed to pair you with someone and keep dates safe. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.
  • Video and sound on a date pass between the two phones through our relay, encrypted. We do not record or store your dates.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

Passionfruit is a live video speed dating app for iPhone, and this website with its waitlist. This page covers both. "We" means Seymur Mammadov, who operates Passionfruit. To reach us about anything here, write to support@passionfruitdating.com.

What happens during a date

  • Video and sound travel between your phone and the other person's phone through a relay we run. They are encrypted between the two phones, we cannot watch or listen, we do not record them and they are not stored. Because the call goes through the relay, the other person's phone is not given your network address; the relay sees it for as long as the call lasts, as any server you connect to does.
  • Nudity check. While you are on a date, a still frame from your own camera is checked for nudity every few seconds. The check runs on your phone. The picture is not kept. If the check finds nudity, the result is sent to our server, the date ends for both people, and a strike is added to the account.
  • Language check. What you say is turned into text on your phone. The text is sent to our server, checked there for sexual or abusive language, and then discarded. We keep a record that a check failed, not the words.
  • The app tells you about both checks and asks for your agreement before your first date, and shows a "Safety check on" mark during every date.

Calendar

If you add an agreed date to your calendar, the app asks for permission to add events only. It cannot read your calendar, and nothing from it is sent to us.

The helper in your chats

Each match has a small character that can suggest things, such as a reply or a place to meet. When it looks up a place, the search words and area are sent to Apple Maps.

Smarter chat help (off until you turn it on). The first time you open a chat, the app asks whether to turn it on. If you say yes, then while you have a chat open, the last few messages in it, yours and your match's, together with first names, ages, profile text and usual free hours for the two of you, are sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service, to write the suggestion. We do not send account ids, email addresses, photos or locations. Anthropic processes the text to produce the answer; we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Choosing what to suggest. With smarter chat help on for both of you, the last eight messages of the open chat and your two first names are also sent to TypeSafe, a classification service, each time a new message arrives. It answers only with which kind of suggestion fits (a place, a time, a call, a question) and, if a day, time or kind of food was mentioned, which one. It writes nothing, and we do not let it use the text for anything else.

Turning it off. You can turn smarter chat help off at any time in Settings (the cog on your profile). The character then works on your phone using Apple's on-device model, and your messages are not sent to an outside AI service, for your own suggestions or for your match's. If you answer "Not now" when the app asks, it stays off for you until you turn it on.

If your match has not turned it on. Smarter chat help does not work in that chat for you either: a chat is sent only when both of you have said yes.

What your match can see. In a chat, each of you can see whether the other has smarter chat help turned on, so that a chat without suggestions explains itself. Nothing else about your settings is shown.

Notes the character keeps. With smarter chat help on, the character keeps short notes about each chat on our server (for example a plan you made, or a food someone likes) so its suggestions fit. It does not keep contact details, last names, or anything about health, money, politics or religion. Beside the notes it keeps how warm the chat seemed (a number), where it stands (one word, such as "talking"), and the day and hour of a date you agreed, so it can offer help at the right moment. You can read the notes on your match's profile and erase them there. Things you ask it to remember are shown to you only. The notes are deleted when the match or your account is deleted.

Safety notes. If a message suggests that someone is under 18 or is asking for money, a note is recorded for a person on our team to review. The words of the message are not stored in that note, and nothing happens to an account automatically.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data is kept while you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, preferences, matches and messages are deleted. A Premium subscription bought through Apple is not cancelled by deleting the account; cancel it in your Apple account settings.
  • We may keep the minimum needed to honour blocks and bans, and to meet legal duties, after an account is deleted.
  • Waitlist entries are kept until Passionfruit has opened in your borough and you have been told, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your profile, tap the cog, then Delete account.
  • Leave the waitlist, or ask what we hold about you: write to support@passionfruitdating.com from the address you signed up with.
  • Camera, microphone, speech and location can be switched off in iPhone Settings. Dates need the camera and microphone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to see, correct or delete your data. Write to us and we will act on it.

Age

Passionfruit is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, report them in the app or write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app or on this page before the change takes effect.
