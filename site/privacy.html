Privacy policy

In effect from 16 November 2026.

The short version

  • We collect what is needed to pair you with someone and keep dates safe. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.
  • Video and sound on a date go between the two phones. We do not record or store your dates.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

Passionfruit is a live video speed dating app for iPhone, and this website with its waitlist. This page covers both. "We" means Passionfruit. To reach us about anything here, write to support@passionfruitdating.com.

What happens during a date

  • Video and sound travel between your phone and the other person's phone. We do not record them and they are not stored on our servers. As with any direct call, the two phones exchange network addresses to connect.
  • Nudity check. While you are on a date, a still frame from your own camera is checked for nudity every few seconds. The check runs on your phone. The picture is not kept. If the check finds nudity, the result is sent to our server, the date ends for both people, and a strike is added to the account.
  • Language check. What you say is turned into text on your phone and checked for sexual language. We keep a record that a check failed, not the words.
  • The app tells you about both checks and asks for your agreement before your first date, and shows a "Safety check on" mark during every date.

The helper in your chats

Each match has a small character that can suggest things, such as a place to meet. It runs on your phone using Apple's on-device model. Your messages are not sent to an outside AI service. When it looks up a place, the search words and area are sent to Apple Maps.

What we collect on this website

  • Waitlist. Your email address and ZIP code, and the borough the ZIP is in. We use them to tell you once when Passionfruit opens in your borough and to show how many people have joined in each borough. The map shows one dot per person placed at random inside their borough, never an address or a ZIP code.
  • Network address. Kept for up to an hour to stop one address from flooding the form.
  • Your browser stores a small note on your own device so the page remembers you joined. The site uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data is kept while you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, preferences, matches and messages are deleted.
  • We may keep the minimum needed to honour blocks and bans, and to meet legal duties, after an account is deleted.
  • Waitlist entries are kept until Passionfruit has opened in your borough and you have been told, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open You, then Account, then Delete account.
  • Leave the waitlist, or ask what we hold about you: write to support@passionfruitdating.com from the address you signed up with.
  • Camera, microphone, speech and location can be switched off in iPhone Settings. Dates need the camera and microphone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to see, correct or delete your data. Write to us and we will act on it.

Age

Passionfruit is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, report them in the app or write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app or on this page before the change takes effect.
